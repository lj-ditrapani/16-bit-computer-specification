single bus that can be segmented in two
Instructions take either 2 or 3 cycles of 279 ns to execute.
- 1. Fetch
- 2. execute
- 3. memory read/write (only for LOD or STR instructions)
DRAM refresh can happen on execute cycles driven by cpu.
The VDP can read VRAM when the bus is segmented. This happens during rendering.
Max pin-out per chip is 44 or 46 pins.

W = 16-bit word
1 W = 2B
1 KW = 2 KB
A bus = 16 wire address lines
D bus = 16 wire data lines
EN = chip enable input, driven by CPU 8 device enable lines
I = vertical blank CPU interrupt (from VDP to CPU & segmenter)

Data RAM is made of 2 chips of NEC µPD43256 (or Hitachi HM62256) 150 ns SRAM 32K x 8-bit wired in parallel to produce a 32K x 16-bit memory.
This would have likely cost around $30 USD in bulk at the start of 1985.
Video RAM is made of 2 chips of Hitachi HM6264 150 ns SRAM 8K x 8-bit wired in parallel to produce a 8K x 16-bit memory.
This would have likely cost around $8 USD in bulk at the start of 1985.
All the SRAM chips are 28-pin dip chips.

The VDP passes-through the vram enable input when not rendering.
The VDP ignores the 2 enable inputs when rendering.
The VDP takes control of the vram via the vram enable output when rendering.
